# NuCatch App - Complete User Guide

## Welcome to NuCatch!
This guide will walk you through all features of the NuCatch mathematical game application.

---

## 📱 Main Menu Overview

When you open NuCatch, you'll see the main menu with three primary options:

1. **Start** - Begin playing Solo or Combat mode
2. **Top Score** - View leaderboards and personal records
3. **Settings** - Customize your profile and preferences

---

## 🎮 1. Start - Game Modes

The **Start** button opens the Play Mode selection screen where you can choose between two exciting game modes:

### 1.1 Solo Mode - Play Alone

**What is Solo Mode?**
Solo Mode is a single-player mathematical challenge where you solve equations as quickly as possible. Test your skills, improve your speed, and climb the leaderboard!

**How to Play Solo Mode:**

#### 1.1.1 Select Difficulty Level

After choosing Solo Mode, you'll be prompted to select a difficulty level. Each difficulty affects:
- **Equation Complexity** - Number range and operations
- **Time Limit** - How long you have to answer
- **Score Multiplier** - Higher difficulty = more points

**Difficulty Options:**

##### 🟢 Easy
- **Number Range:** 1-10
- **Operations:** Addition (+) and Subtraction (-)
- **Time per Question:** 15 seconds
- **Score Multiplier:** 1x
- **Best For:** Beginners and warm-up practice

##### 🟡 Medium
- **Number Range:** 1-50
- **Operations:** +, -, Multiplication (×)
- **Time per Question:** 12 seconds
- **Score Multiplier:** 1.5x
- **Best For:** Intermediate players

##### 🟠 Hard
- **Number Range:** 1-100
- **Operations:** +, -, ×, Division (÷)
- **Time per Question:** 10 seconds
- **Score Multiplier:** 2x
- **Best For:** Advanced players

##### 🔴 Extremely Hard
- **Number Range:** 1-200
- **Operations:** All operations with complex combinations
- **Time per Question:** 8 seconds
- **Score Multiplier:** 3x
- **Best For:** Expert mathematicians

#### 1.1.2 Start Playing

Once you've selected your difficulty:

1. **Equation Appears** - A mathematical equation is displayed
2. **Type Your Answer** - Use the on-screen keyboard to enter the result
3. **Submit** - Tap the check button or press enter
4. **Feedback:**
   - ✅ **Correct:** Score increases, next equation appears
   - ❌ **Wrong:** Lose a life, try the next equation
   - ⏱️ **Timeout:** Lose a life if time runs out

5. **Game Continues** - Keep solving equations until you run out of lives
6. **Game Over** - View your final score and compare with your best

**Lives System:**
- You start with 3 lives
- Each wrong answer or timeout costs 1 life
- Game ends when all lives are lost

---

### 1.2 Combat Mode - Multiplayer via Bluetooth

**What is Combat Mode?**
Combat Mode is a **real-time multiplayer** mathematical battle where two players compete head-to-head using **Bluetooth connection**. No WiFi or internet required!

**Key Features:**
- ⚡ **Turn-Based Gameplay** - Players alternate solving equations
- 📡 **Bluetooth Only** - Works offline using Nearby Connections
- 🏆 **Competitive** - Race to outscore your opponent
- 📍 **Local Play** - Devices must be within 10 meters

---

#### 1.2.1 Create Room (Host)

**How to Host a Game:**

1. **Tap "Create Room"** on Combat Mode Setup screen
2. **Grant Permissions** (if first time):
   - Bluetooth permission
   - Location permission (required for Nearby Connections)
3. **Room Created** - Your device becomes the host
4. **Room Code Displayed** - Share this code with your opponent
5. **Wait for Guest** - Screen shows "Waiting for opponent to join..."
6. **Guest Joins** - You'll see their name appear
7. **Tap "Ready"** - Confirm you're ready to play
8. **Wait for Guest Ready** - Both players must be ready
9. **Select Difficulty** - As host, you choose the difficulty level
10. **Game Starts** - You play first as the host!

**Host Responsibilities:**
- ✅ Create and manage the room
- ✅ Choose difficulty level
- ✅ Start first in the initial match
- ✅ Can disconnect to end the game

---

#### 1.2.2 Join Room (Guest)

**How to Join a Game:**

1. **Tap "Join Room"** on Combat Mode Setup screen
2. **Grant Permissions** (if first time):
   - Bluetooth permission
   - Location permission
3. **Scanning** - App searches for nearby rooms
4. **Select Room** - Choose the host's room from the list
5. **Connected** - You'll see the host's name
6. **Tap "Ready"** - Confirm you're ready to play
7. **Wait for Host** - Host must also tap ready
8. **Wait for Difficulty** - Host selects the difficulty
9. **Game Starts** - Host plays first, you play second

**Guest Notes:**
- ⏳ Wait for host to choose difficulty
- 👀 You can see the host's progress in real-time
- 🎮 You play second in the initial match

---

#### 1.2.3 Both Players Ready - Game Setup

**What Happens After Both Players Tap "Ready":**

##### 1.2.3.1 Guest Waits for Host

**Guest Screen:**
- Shows "Waiting for host to select difficulty..."
- Displays host's name and status
- Cannot proceed until host chooses

**What to Do:**
- ✅ Wait patiently
- 👀 Watch for difficulty selection
- 🎮 Get ready to play!

##### 1.2.3.2 Host Selects Difficulty

**Host Screen:**
- Shows difficulty selection screen
- Same options as Solo Mode (Easy, Medium, Hard, Extremely Hard)
- Guest sees your selection in real-time

**Host Steps:**
1. Review difficulty options
2. Choose appropriate level for both players
3. Tap to confirm selection
4. Game starts immediately!

---

#### 1.2.4 Combat Gameplay - Turn-Based System

**How Combat Mode Works:**

**Initial Match:**
- 🎯 **Host plays first** - Host solves the first equation
- 🔄 **Guest plays second** - Guest solves the next equation
- ♻️ **Turns alternate** - Players take turns throughout the game

**Turn Mechanics:**
1. **Your Turn:**
   - Equation appears on your screen
   - Timer starts counting down
   - Type your answer using the keyboard
   - Submit before time runs out

2. **Opponent's Turn:**
   - You see their equation (read-only)
   - Watch their typing progress in real-time
   - See if they answer correctly or incorrectly
   - Get ready for your next turn

3. **Scoring:**
   - ✅ Correct answer: +Points (based on difficulty)
   - ❌ Wrong answer: -1 Life
   - ⏱️ Timeout: -1 Life
   - 🏆 First to lose all lives loses the match

**Real-Time Synchronization:**
- 📊 Both players see each other's scores
- ❤️ Both players see each other's remaining lives
- ⌨️ Guest sees host's typing progress
- 🔄 Smooth turn transitions

**Re-Match System:**
- 🔁 After a match ends, players can choose to rematch
- 🎲 **Important:** In a rematch, the **loser plays first**
- ⚖️ This gives the losing player a chance to catch up
- 🔄 Turn order continues alternating from there

**Winning Conditions:**
- 🏆 Opponent runs out of lives
- 🚪 Opponent disconnects
- ⏰ Opponent fails to answer in time repeatedly

## 📡 Bluetooth Connection Tips

**For Best Combat Mode Experience:**

✅ **Do:**
- Keep devices within 10 meters (33 feet)
- Enable Bluetooth before starting
- Grant all requested permissions
- Play in open areas (fewer obstacles)
- Ensure both devices have battery

❌ **Don't:**
- Move too far apart during gameplay
- Play through thick walls
- Disable Bluetooth mid-game
- Force close the app during a match

**Troubleshooting:**
- **Can't find room?** - Check Bluetooth is enabled
- **Connection drops?** - Move closer together
- **Slow response?** - Reduce distance between devices
- **Permission errors?** - Check app settings

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need internet for Combat Mode?**
A: No! Combat Mode uses Bluetooth only, no WiFi or internet required.

**Q: How far apart can players be?**
A: Maximum 10 meters (33 feet) for stable connection.

**Q: Can I play with more than 2 players?**
A: Currently, Combat Mode supports 1v1 only.

**Q: Do scores sync across devices?**
A: Scores are stored locally on your device.

**Q: Can I change difficulty mid-game?**
A: No, difficulty is set before the game starts.
